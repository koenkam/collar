## Manual "The Wheel"

1. I am entering immediately
2. i am selling the cash covered put as soon as the real premium profit exceeds the linear theoretical depreciation of the premium. so if the expiry is 10 days and on day 2 the premium to close the position has decreased by 25%, i close and repeat step 1.
3. I am keeping 30% of capital free to be able to be able to continue to write put options for a stock, even if i have been assigend some stock already. when assigned an additional batch of stocks, the average purchase price goes down, which allows me to sell covered calls at a lower strike.
4. defending a bad put: when the price of the share goes against me and dips below the strike, i roll the put so the strike is lowered, while at the same time collecting additional premium. this may mean a position is moved 2 to 4 weeks out. on a +10 day initial expiry, this is a relative long expiry increase.
